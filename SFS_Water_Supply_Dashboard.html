Built only from this tool's 2025–2026 dataset. Tanker figures use nominal volume (loads × 12,000 L) — the source sheet's own "Actuals" column has a recurring meter-formula glitch and isn't summed. Rainwater and KWA figures exclude flagged/anomalous days (see Daily Log tab); the excluded-day count, if any, is shown below the chart.
Known data caveats in the source sheet (flagged, not hidden): (1) "Tanker Water Actuals" shows broken placeholder values (huge negative/positive numbers) on days adjacent to a missing meter reading — this column is never summed into totals here (nominal tanker volume = loads × 12,000 L is used instead); affected rows are shown with ⚠ in the log. (2) The "Rainwater" column switched units partway through — most of 2025–2026 is a small count of 12,000 L equivalents, but entries from early Sep 2026 onward use raw litres directly; this tool auto-detects which (≤10 ⇒ equivalents, >10 ⇒ already litres) and flags the raw-litre rows. (3) A few days have "pool water" logged in the Rainwater column by mistake (per the sheet's own remarks) — excluded from rainwater totals here. (4) KWA 1 / KWA 2 are shown as raw cumulative meter readings (KL) exactly as recorded — this tool does not attempt to split the daily "KWA Water Received" total back into a per-index (KWA1 vs KWA2) share.
