All days are still counted. Only the wrong value is handled, as below.

  • Tanker meter glitch — "Tanker Water Actuals" sometimes shows impossible numbers. That column is never used; tankers are counted as loads × 12,000 L. Marked ⚠.
  • Rainwater unit change — entered as loads (1, 2, 3…) until Aug 2026, and as litres from Sep 2026. Both are read correctly (10 or less = loads). Shown as a "raw-L format" note.
  • Pool water entered as rain — that day's rainwater figure is left out; the rest of the day is counted. Marked ⚠.
  • Negative KWA value — a negative "KWA Water Received" (a meter-formula error) is left out of the KWA total; the rest of the day is counted. Marked ⚠.
  • KWA meter readings — in KL, and not taken every day. "KWA Water Received" is the water since the previous reading.
